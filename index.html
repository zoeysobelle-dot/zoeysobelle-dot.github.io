About

01 — Background

I'm a mechanical design engineer, four years into a role that started in engineering sales support and grew into running large stainless steel equipment projects end to end — tanks, ducting, and process systems for the dairy and food sectors.

My work sits across three disciplines: designing and detailing fabrication equipment in CAD, acting as the coordinating point of contact across design, third-party verification, and delivery, and building automation tools that remove repetitive work from my own process and my team's.
